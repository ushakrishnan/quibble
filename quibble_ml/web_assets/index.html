Backend limits and verification details

Verify saved evidence — no training

Uses complete full-run predictions, source validation, recorded recipes and the original saved policy. Smoke, failed, excluded or incomplete evidence is refused. No fits, model downloads or provider calls. The bounded check may take up to 120 seconds; job status remains readable.

Swap checks replacement against the saved acceptance policy. Baseline gate checks only improvement by the chosen margin using the saved primary metric. They are not interchangeable. PASS is not production-safety approval.

        Advanced: import named relational tables
        
Separate from flat upload. All tables stay local. Supply a schema-1 manifest with explicit primary/foreign keys, observed-only features, event/availability timestamps, prediction entity, horizon and official train/validation/test assignments. Nothing is inferred silently.

        
Supported routes: CPU HistGB, LightGBM and CatBoost, official holdout only. Flattened features are not a relational-native model. Repeated-entity uncertainty retains a temporal-dependence caveat.

        
        
        
        
        
Use the documented synthetic fixture as a complete example. Declared file paths must be tables/NAME.csv or tables/NAME.parquet and hashes must match the selected bytes. Maximum 16 MB/file, 64 MB combined; full row/feature/join budgets are enforced during validation. No existing dataset can be overwritten.

        

        

        

        
        
      

    

  

  

    
01 / CHOOSE DATA / NEW DESIGN

    
What would you like to predict?

    
Why generate fresh data? Create new rows under explicit rules instead of relying only on familiar public benchmarks that pretrained predictors may have encountered. This reduces reuse of benchmark rows, but does not prove a task is unseen or free from bias. Vary one condition at a time, repeat across generation seeds, and check useful findings on representative real data. You can also use an existing dataset below.

    

    

      
    

    
Binary example: will inventory fall short before the next delivery? Predict 0 (covers demand) or 1 (shortfall).

    
Design mode: describe a new dataset, or choose a ready-made dataset to reuse saved rows.

    
    
    

      
      
      
    

    
Choose classification for “will inventory cover demand: yes/no.” Regression predicts a continuous synthetic score instead, not a yes/no answer or automatically demand units. The selected outcome type takes precedence; review the target meaning and assumptions in the proposed design.

    

    
    

      
    

    
To design your own dataset, enter your scenario and choose Propose a scenario with my provider. Invalid provider output may trigger one correction request; provider charges can apply to both requests. Credentials stay in the server environment.

    02 / METHODS AND SETTINGS
    
    
    Configure the comparison.
    
    
    
    
    
    
      
      
      
    
    
    
    Prediction methods (choose up to four)
    
    
    
    
    
      Comparison tier
      
      
    
        
    
          
          
          
          
          
        
    
        
    Equal trials, not equal compute: 20 trials × 3 inner folds plus refit means 61 fits per tree per outer split. Only outer-training rows are used for selection. The outer worker timeout is authoritative; this optional deadline cannot interrupt a running fit.
    
        
    Only HistGB, LightGBM and CatBoost are searched. Foundation recipes remain defaults or explicitly fine-tuned as recorded by their adapter; tuning trees does not grant model usage rights. Each browser job has its own output root.
    
      
    
    
    
    
    Runs use one worker and model seed 0. Smoke uses at most 128 training and 32 test rows, with no swap approval. CUDA runs exclude incompatible methods rather than running them on CPU.
    
    
    
      Swap acceptance policy (full runs)
      
    These are the default limits Quibble uses, not universally safe acceptance criteria. Review them for your application. Custom JSON overrides only the keys you supply. Smoke runs do not evaluate a swap policy.
    
      
    
        Configure swap thresholds
        
        
        
        
        
    
          
          
        
    
      
    
      
    
      
    Effective limits (defaults plus overrides)
    
      
    The effective policy is saved with each full experiment and shown in its report. Download a copy to reuse with the CLI or import for a later run. Threshold meanings and units.
    
    
    
    
    
      What Full does, and what the results establish
      
    Each selected method fits on training rows and predicts separate held-out test rows. Holdout uses 70% for training and 30% for testing. Three-fold evaluation uses two thirds to train and one third to test, with three fresh fits; two repeats uses six fits per method. Classification preserves class proportions when feasible. Methods share the same splits for paired comparisons.
    
      
    Preprocessing is learned on training rows. Tree methods train on that split; pretrained methods prepare or condition on those examples rather than retraining their foundation weights from scratch. Full removes smoke row caps, but method-specific limits still apply and refusals are reported.
    
      
    The pipeline saves held-out predictions, computes quality, timing, disagreement and slice results, and runs configured swap checks when comparable results exist. Each new browser experiment uses its own output directory. Defaults do not search settings; the opt-in tuned tree tier searches only within outer-training rows. Neither tier is a final deployment fit.
    
      
    Results measure behavior under this dataset, split scheme, settings, and hardware. They do not prove universal superiority, deployment readiness, causal validity, or absence of pretraining exposure. Repeated folds reuse the same table; independently generated tables and representative real data are separate checks. Do not repeatedly tune against the final test outcomes.
